Dear Jaanaa…

Teachers' Day is supposed to be about thanking the people who taught us something. So, technically, I'm allowed to be here — you've taught me more than you probably realize. Patience. Softness. How to argue about nothing and laugh about everything five minutes later. How a home is really just wherever you are, rearranged.

I love the way your smile shows up before you even say anything funny. I love your little habits — the ones you think are annoying and I secretly find unbearably charming. I love the way you can run a room full of students and still come home and pretend you don't know how brilliant you are.

We've built a small stack of memories together, and somehow every one of them has your laugh in the background. I don't think I ever get used to that sound. I still don't think I deserve how lucky I am — but I've decided to stop questioning it and just enjoy it.

So today isn't about the students you shaped, or the classroom you run like a small, elegant empire. Today is about the one person you've taught the most — the one currently writing you a slightly embarrassing love letter in HTML.

Happy Teachers' Day to my favorite teacher, my favorite person, and my forever Jaanaa. ❤️

Forever yours ❤️
